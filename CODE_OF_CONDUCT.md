# Community standards

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming to beginners. Critique code rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. Moderation should be consistent and allow clarification when practical.

For concerns, contact @ascebiswajit through a contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive reports in public issues. Do not assume a private reporting channel exists unless one is explicitly listed.
